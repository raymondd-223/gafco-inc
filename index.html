Who we are

A contractor that answers its own phone

GAFCO Construction is a full service, self performing general contracting firm. Our company is a woman owned corporation and is in the process of obtaining minority status.

Helene Frudakis is the owner of GAFCO and head of the design center. George Frudakis, head of construction, has over 32 years in the industry and has completed work for Raytheon, Boeing, Mattel and DIRECTV, to name a few. Every project manager, field supervisor and laborer is hand picked by the owners.

Excellent construction work depends on reliable communication, prompt response and meticulous attention to detail. Effective and timely communication is our number one priority on every job, and it is the reason our clients keep coming back.

What we do

Comprehensive services, performed in house

One of the principals of GAFCO has over 32 years of experience in construction. Combined with the project manager, superintendent, field foreman and crew leader, there is an overall construction experience of over 100 years on your project.

Pre construction

Planning, accurate budgeting and estimating, value engineering analysis and on time scheduling before a shovel moves.

Design build

In house architectural design carried straight through to permitting and final completion, with one point of accountability.

Construction management

Detailed management with weekly staff meetings on long lead items, schedule conflicts, manpower and client issues.

Self performing trades

Concrete, steel stud framing, drywall and finish, acoustic ceilings and walls, painting, cabinetry and tiling by our own crews.

Tenant improvement

Corporate floors, labs, lobbies and data systems delivered around live operations for Fortune 500 tenants.

Safety and compliance

A written safety manual, a dedicated safety manager on site inspections, and a program that meets and exceeds OSHA requirements.
